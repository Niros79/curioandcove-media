Christmas Gift Ideas for Travelers 2026: Useful Gifts They'll Actually Use

Published October 17, 2026 – 5-minute read

Buying for someone who travels is harder than it looks: they pack light, they already own the obvious gadgets, and the last thing they need is more stuff. These are gifts travelers actually use, from experiences to things you can send by email on December 24th.

1. Gifts that are experiences

The best travel gifts are often the ones you can't wrap. If you know where they're going next year, book something they'd love but might not splurge on themselves:

  • Tickets to a museum, show or landmark. Tiqets sells timed tickets for thousands of attractions worldwide, delivered to your phone.
  • A city pass for a city with lots of paid sights. Go City bundles dozens of attractions into one pass; our guide to whether city passes are worth it shows how to check before you buy.
  • A guided tour or food tour. Browse tours and activities on Trip.com and choose one with free cancellation in case their plans change.

Tip: print the booking confirmation and put it in a card, or wrap a small guidebook with it. It turns a QR code into a real present.

2. Gifts for the tech-savvy traveler

Subscriptions are perfect for people who "have everything", because they use them every day.

  • A VPN for hotel and airport Wi-Fi. NordVPN is the established all-rounder; Surfshark covers unlimited devices, so one subscription can protect the whole family. Not sure they need one? Here's our honest VPN guide.
  • A password manager. NordPass stores logins safely and fills them in on any device, which matters more when you're logging in from abroad.
  • Mobile data for their next trip. Offer to cover a travel eSIM such as Saily and set it up together before they leave. Our eSIM comparison explains how it works.

Buying in late November? These are among the deepest discounts of the year; see which Black Friday deals are worth it.

3. Gifts for the homebody who dreams of travel

For the person who loves cooking, plants or simply staying cozy in winter, a Click & Grow smart garden is a lovely gift: plant pods go in, the built-in light and water tank do the rest, and they get fresh herbs on the kitchen counter all winter. It's an easy win for beginners, and our windowsill herb guide covers which herbs to start with.

Pair it with a blank travel journal and a few of our journaling prompts for a thoughtful, personal touch.

4. Gifts for road-trippers

A dash cam records the road while they drive, which helps with insurance claims and disputes after an accident, and captures the scenery on long road trips. Rexing makes dash cams in different sizes and budgets, including front-and-rear models. Check local rules before using one abroad, since a few countries restrict recording.

See Rexing dash cams

5. Stocking stuffers under $25

Small, useful and easy to pack (no links needed, any good shop will have them):

  • A universal travel adapter with USB ports
  • Packing cubes or a set of compression bags
  • A compact power bank
  • A luggage tracker tag
  • A collapsible water bottle
  • A sleep mask and good earplugs for flights

6. Last-minute digital gifts

Forgot someone? These can be bought and sent on the same day, even on Christmas Eve:

  • Attraction tickets on Tiqets, delivered by email
  • A subscription to NordVPN, Surfshark or NordPass
  • A promise card for a trip you'll plan together, plus our trip planning checklist to get started

How to choose the right gift

  1. Ask about their next trip. A destination makes experience gifts easy.
  2. Think daily use. The best travel gifts get used every week, not once a year.
  3. Check dates and refund rules. Choose flexible tickets and subscriptions with a money-back period.

Prices, availability and offers change, especially around Black Friday and Christmas. Check the current details on each company's website before you buy.
