Privacy policy

Last updated: October 1, 2026

This policy explains what information is collected when you visit curioandcove.com and how it is used. Curio & Cove is operated from Romania, so it follows the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) for all visitors.

Who is responsible

The data controller is Curio & Cove. You can reach us at hello@curioandcove.com.

What we collect

We do not ask you to create an account and this site has no comment section. Our hosting provider, Cloudflare, processes technical data such as your IP address and browser type to deliver the site securely and protect it from attacks. If you email us, we use your address and message only to reply.

Analytics

We use Cloudflare Web Analytics to count visits and see which pages are read most. It does not use cookies, does not follow you across other websites and does not collect personal details such as your name or email address. Cloudflare processes basic technical information, such as the page you visited, your browser type and your approximate country, to produce combined statistics.

Affiliate links and cookies

When you click an affiliate link, you leave our site. The affiliate network (for example Digistore24 or CJ) or the seller may set cookies on their own website to record that you came from us, so a purchase can be credited. Those cookies are controlled by them and covered by their own privacy policies. This website itself does not set advertising or tracking cookies.

Some travel articles include search boxes and price widgets from travel partners in the Travelpayouts network, such as flight price calendars, flight search, eSIM search and airport transfer search. These widgets are loaded from the partner's own servers (tpwidg.com) and may use cookies or similar technologies to show prices and to record that a booking came from our site. They are controlled by those providers and covered by their own privacy policies. If you prefer, you can block third-party cookies in your browser settings; the rest of the article will still work.

Pinterest

If you follow links from Pinterest or save our images to Pinterest, Pinterest processes data under its own privacy policy.

Newsletter

If you subscribe to our newsletter, we collect your email address to send you the free checklist and our emails. Subscriptions are managed by Kit (kit.com), which stores your address on our behalf. You can unsubscribe at any time using the link at the bottom of every email, and we'll stop emailing you. We never sell or share your email address.

Your rights

Under GDPR you can ask to access, correct or delete personal data we hold about you, object to its use, or complain to a supervisory authority. In Romania this is the National Supervisory Authority for Personal Data Processing (ANSPDCP). To make a request, email hello@curioandcove.com.

Changes

If we add features such as a newsletter, we will update this page before they go live.
